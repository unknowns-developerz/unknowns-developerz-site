Privacy.

1. Summary

Time Manager is an Android screen-time awareness and planning app developed by Unknowns Developerz. Its core purpose is to show usage patterns, organize app activity into useful categories, generate optional AI-based reflections, and let users create time budgets.

The app is designed around usage metadata, not personal content. Time Manager does not need to read your messages, photos, files, passwords, or the contents displayed inside other apps.

2. What Time Manager accesses

With Android Usage Access permission, Time Manager can read usage statistics made available by Android. Depending on the device and Android version, this can include:

  • app/package names;
  • how long apps were used;
  • timestamps or usage events needed to calculate screen-time patterns;
  • derived category totals, daily totals, comparisons, and time-budget progress.

These statistics are used to provide the Today, Insights, and Limits experiences.

3. What we do not read

Time Manager is not designed to access the content inside your apps. We do not read:

  • message or email contents;
  • photos, videos, or personal files;
  • passwords, form contents, or keystrokes;
  • the contents of your screen;
  • your contacts solely for the purpose of screen-time analytics.

If a future feature requires a new type of data, this policy and the app's permission flow will be updated before that feature is relied upon.

4. Smart app categorization

Time Manager groups apps into functional categories so that raw package usage becomes easier to understand. Known category mappings are cached and reused so the same app does not need to be classified repeatedly.

When an app is not already known, the app may send limited app metadata—such as the package name and application display name—to the Time Manager backend so that a category can be resolved. An AI service may be used for previously unknown apps. The resulting category mapping may be stored in the backend and locally cached for future requests.

Categorization is about identifying what an app is, not reading what you do inside it.

5. AI Insights

AI Insights are optional and user-triggered. The app does not need to generate an AI reflection every time you open Insights.

When you explicitly request an AI Insight, Time Manager may send the minimum usage summary needed to generate that response, such as aggregated screen-time totals, category-level usage, changes, trends, goals, or similar derived analytics. Personal message contents, photos, files, and screen contents are not required for this feature.

7. Storage and retention

Usage analytics needed by the app may be stored or cached locally on your device. Reusable app-category mappings may also be stored locally and in the backend so classification becomes faster and does not need to be repeated for every user or every launch.

We do not intentionally store the personal content types listed in section 3 because Time Manager does not need them for its core functionality.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures intended to protect data transmitted to our backend and to limit unnecessary collection. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure, so we continuously aim to reduce the amount of data that needs to leave the device in the first place.

9. Children's privacy

Time Manager is not designed as a service directed specifically to children under the minimum age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information to us in a way that requires action, contact us using the address below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Time Manager's features, permissions, infrastructure, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected on this page by updating the effective date.
